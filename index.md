      Hi, my name's Riddhi (<a href="/assets/img/name_in_bengali.png" target="_blank">my name in Bengali</a>), and welcome to my online space! I am currently a Research Scientist III, in the Data and Analytics Division within the Epidemiological Research Section, at the <a href="https://www.cdph.ca.gov/Programs/CHCQ/Pages/CHCQHome.aspx" target="_blank">Center for Health Care Quality</a>, <a href="https://www.cdph.ca.gov/" target="_blank">California Department of Public Health</a>. I am also a  <a href="https://www.marquette.edu/" target="_blank">Marquette</a> alumnus, an <a href="https://www.buet.ac.bd/web/#/" target="_blank">ex-BUETian</a>, and an ex-cadet from <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jhenaidah_Cadet_College" target="_blank">JCC</a> in Bangladesh.
      <br><br>
      I have 5+ years of research experience in Machine Learning & Data Science for healthcare; as well as software development & engineering. I have had 15+ publications and service as reviewer in 10+ conferences and journals in the Healthcare and Machine Learning domain. Currently, I am actively working in the field of <b><i>Causal Inference</i></b>; my primary focus is to apply Causal Inference and related theories in exploring cause-and-effect relationships in experimental and observational datasets. For more of my professional experiences, visit my <a href="pages/experience.html" target="_blank">experience page</a>.
      <br><br>
      My research goals aim to achieve <b><i>data-driven causal explorations</i></b>, mostly for <b><i>Electronic Healthcare Data</i></b> My primary tools are <b><i>Structural Causal Models</i></b>, <b><i>Propensity Score Matching</i></b>, <b><i>A/B Testing</i></b> and a little bit of <b><i>Do-Why</i></b>. For more, visit my <a href="pages/research.html" target="_blank">research page</a>.

      Outside research activities, I destress mostly by singing. My other hobbies include playing guitar, reading thriller books, playing board games, PS5 games & basketball, and some more. For more, visit my <a href="pages/hobbies.html" target="_blank">hobbies page</a>.
